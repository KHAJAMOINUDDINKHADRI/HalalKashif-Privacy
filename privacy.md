# Privacy Policy for Halal Kashif

**Effective Date: January 17, 2026**

Halal Kashif ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the Halal Kashif mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect

Halal Kashif is designed to be a privacy-first application. Most data used by the app is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us.

*   **Scan History:** The app maintains a temporary local record of your most recent product scans for your convenience. This history is stored only on your device.
*   **Favorites:** When you explicitly save a product as a "Favorite", this data is stored in your local database until you choose to remove it.
*   **Device Data:** We may store an anonymous, unique device identifier locally to manage offline database updates and sync status.
*   **Camera Access:** Required for barcode scanning. We do not transmit, store, or share any data from your camera feed. The camera is used solely to detect and read barcodes.

## 2. Data Storage & Privacy

All product scan history and favorite items are stored **locally on your device**. We do not collect, upload, or sell your personal scan history or favorites list to our servers or any third parties. Your data remains under your control.

## 3. Third-Party Services

When you search for a product that is not in the local database, the app may query a third-party product API (such as Open Food Facts) to retrieve information. In these cases:
*   Only the barcode or search term is sent to the service.
*   No personal information, account data, or unique device identifiers are shared with these services.
*   These third-party services have their own privacy policies which you may wish to review.

## 4. Data Deletion

You can clear your scan history or remove items from your favorites at any time within the app settings. Since all data is stored locally, deleting the application from your device will also permanently remove all locally stored data.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Our services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
